About

I'm Alwin. I started Shutterhaus Visuals in 2019 and shoot on a Canon 4000D, mostly out in daylight rather than under studio strobes.

Most of what I photograph is people. Couples, families, and anyone who wants one honest picture of themselves instead of an hour of holding a pose. There is event and commercial work too, and I travel across South Africa when the brief is right.

If you don't know what you want, tell me what the photos are for and I'll tell you what I'd do.

Services

Portraits

Individuals, couples and families. Usually outdoors, wherever the light is doing something worth having, and unhurried enough that nobody feels rushed.

Events

Weddings, parties and functions. I work quietly, keep out of the way, and cover the room instead of chasing shots.

Commercial

Product, brand and editorial work for businesses that need pictures for a website, a social feed or a campaign.

How it works

  1. 01Enquire
    Tell me what you want photographed, the date, and where.
  2. 02Plan
    We agree on timing, location and what you want to walk away with.
  3. 03Shoot
    Direction where it helps, space where it doesn't. Nobody is pushed.
  4. 04Deliver
    A curated, edited gallery, sent over as a link you can download.
